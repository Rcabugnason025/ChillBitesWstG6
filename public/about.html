About ChillBites

Simple. Authentic. Delicious.

The Story

ChillBites started as a student project with a simple goal: to reimagine how we experience local food delivery. What began as a coding assignment has evolved into a fully functional platform designed to make ordering your favorite Filipino comfort foods as easy as a few clicks.

We focus on the essentials: great food, easy ordering, and reliable service. No complicated menus, no hidden fees—just the meals you love, ready for pickup.

Our Kitchen

We partner with local cooks who know that the best ingredient is always passion.

Mission

To provide a seamless digital ordering experience that connects food lovers with authentic local flavors, prioritizing convenience without compromising quality.

Vision

To become the go-to platform for quick, reliable, and delicious food pickup, setting a new standard for student-led tech innovations in the food service industry.

Core Values

Simplicity

We keep things simple. Easy to use, easy to order.

Passion
